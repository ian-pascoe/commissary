They can also be combined, e.g. `[toggle, effort]` on `claude-sonnet-5`, or `[effort, budget_tokens]` on `claude-opus-4-6`. ## 5. How membership is decided today (`src/registry/credential-models.ts` `baseModels`)

- A credential's models come from its provider's catalog **section**, then `applyExcludedModels` (`excluded-models` wildcards), OAuth model aliases (`oauth-model-alias`), OAuth settings (`max-context-length`), and prefixes (`force-model-prefix`). A credential with no models after that registers nothing.
- Config-defined models (`ModelSource.models`) replace the section for gemini, vertex, claude, xai, meta; Codex API keys build their list from config only (`buildCodexConfigModels`); compat providers are config-only; OpenCode uses `defaultCatalogModels` (models.dev, minus deprecated and, for Zen, free models).
- **Codex OAuth**: the plan tier maps through `CODEX_TIERS`: pro → `codex-pro`, plus → `codex-plus`, team/business/go → `codex-team`, free → `codex-free`, unknown → `codex-pro`.
- **Antigravity**: the static section intersected with the `fetchAvailableModels` entitlements (a legacy reply without `models` never revokes a model).
- Kimi, aistudio, devin, meta (without config) use a single section; Devin uses `devin_models.json` (else the `devin` section, else `staticDevin`) with `devin/swe-1-6-slow` built in.
- Built-ins are always upserted: gpt-image-* into every Codex tier, grok-imagine/TTS into xai.
- Per-model availability (suspension, cooldown, quota) is projected on top (`availability.ts`); `/v1/models` lists models with at least one usable credential.
